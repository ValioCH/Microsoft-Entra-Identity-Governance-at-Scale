## Evidence requirements

- Record only evidence actually captured from the scoped clean-room environment.
- Use sanitized labels instead of operational identifiers.
- A design document or wizard screen does not prove successful configuration.
- A configured control is not **Verified** until its expected and observed behavior are recorded.
- Keep raw exports outside Git until sanitization is complete.
